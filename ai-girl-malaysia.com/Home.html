Sugus.ai FAQ

What is Sugus.ai?

Sugus.ai is an AI companion platform where you can create personalised virtual characters or chat instantly with our featured AI characters in immersive, private conversations — all within a safe and private space.

Can I create my own AI girlfriend?

Yes. The creation flow lets you customise appearance, age range, personality and voice, so your companion matches exactly what you have in mind.

Can my companion send images, voice or video?

Absolutely. Your companion supports chat, voice and AI image generation, and she can respond in the medium that fits the moment.

Do I need to install anything to talk to my AI GF?

No installation needed. You can access Sugus.ai directly from your desktop or mobile browser, anytime and anywhere.

Is chatting with my AI girlfriend private and secure?

Yes. Your conversations are kept private to your account. Your chat history stays under your control and is never shared or sold.
