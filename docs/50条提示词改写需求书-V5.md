## 四、五个维度的具体写法（关键）

### 4.1 `camera` —— 镜头怎么动

**只描述摄像机，不描述人物。** 用独立的一句话，放在运动描述之后。

| 取值 | 必须写成 | 禁止写成 |
|---|---|---|
| `static` | `The camera holds a static shot.` | `camera slowly moves` |
| `slow_push_in` | `The camera pushes in slowly with small amplitude.` | `zooms in on her face`（暗示改变取景） |
| `slow_pull_out` | `The camera pulls back slowly with small amplitude.` | `reveals the full room`（暗示新增内容） |
| `gentle_orbit` | `The camera orbits slowly around her with small amplitude.` | `the camera circles to show her back` |
| `handheld_sway` | `The camera holds with gentle handheld sway.` | `shaky camera` |
| `low_angle_rise` | `The camera rises slowly from a low angle with small amplitude.` | `camera looks up at her from below` |

> **重要**：所有镜头运动都要带 `with small amplitude`（static 除外）。幅度写大了模型会重新构图，导致人物漂移。

### 4.2 `intensity` —— 动作幅度

这是本轮最关键的维度。**必须让三档之间有明显的语言强度差**。

| 取值 | 运动描述应包含 | 参考句式 |
|---|---|---|
| `subtle` | 只有呼吸、眼睑、嘴唇、极轻微重心变化 | `Her breathing deepens and her eyelids lower slowly.` / `She holds her pose with only the faintest rise and fall of breathing.` |
| `moderate` | 明确的身体移动、转头、手部动作、身体起伏 | `Her shoulders shift and her hips begin a steady rocking motion.` / `One hand moves slowly across her body while her breathing quickens.` |
| `intense` | 明显的律动、加快的节奏、强烈的呼吸变化 | `Her body moves with a fast steady rhythm, breathing quick and shallow.` / `Her upper body rocks with increasing force as her breathing turns ragged.` |

**判断标准**：读这句话，能不能想象出画面在「动」？
- subtle 的画面可能是静止的（只有呼吸）
- moderate 的画面必须有可见位移
- intense 的画面必须有明显重复律动

### 4.3 `wardrobe` —— 着装动作

| 取值 | 写成 | 注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `keep` | 不提及衣着变化 | 不要写「clothing stays the same」，多余 |
| `tease` | `One hand moves slowly along the edge of her clothing.` / `She adjusts a strap or edge of fabric slowly.` | 只触碰，不脱 |
| `remove_partial` | `She slowly slides one strap or edge of fabric downward.` | 滑落一部分 |
| `remove_all` | `She slowly draws the fabric fully away from her body.` | 完全脱下 |

**必须加条件句**：`If clothing allows,`（因为输入图可能本来就没穿）
示例：`If clothing allows, she slowly slides one strap or edge of fabric downward.`

### 4.4 `emotion` —— 表情与氛围

用**具体动作**表达情绪，不要用抽象形容词（`sexy` / `hot` 这类无效）。

| 取值 | 写成 |
|---|---|
| `shy` | `She glances slightly aside, then returns her gaze; lips part tentatively.` |
| `seductive` | `She holds direct eye contact, eyelids lowering with a slow breath.` |
| `longing` | `Her gaze softens and drifts, breathing slow and deep.` |
| `ecstatic` | `Her eyes close briefly, head tilting back as breathing turns uneven.` |
| `playful` | `A faint smile forms as she tilts her head slightly.` |
| `detached` | `Her expression stays calm and distant, eyes half-closed.` |

### 4.5 `focus` —— 视觉重心

**这是强调，不是重新取景。** 不要要求镜头「对准某个部位」，只用光线/注意力暗示。

| 取值 | 写法 |
|---|---|
| `face` | `Soft light falls across her face.` |
| `upper_body` | `Soft light falls across her upper body.` |
| `full_body` | `The full figure stays within frame.` |

> 已从维度中移除 `detail`（要求镜头凑近局部），因为 I2V 无法重新取景，风险高。

```

Hands and fingers remain anatomically correct with five fingers each; facial features stay consistent and natural.

overall_soundscape: <与运动强度匹配的环境音>

non_diegetic_music: N/A

### 长度

| 项 | 限制 |
|---|---|
| 全文四段合计 | **≤ 900 字符** |
| `integrated_multimodal_description` | 650 字符 |

### 固定不动的部分

以下两句**每条都必须逐字包含**，不要改写：

```
Maintain the exact composition, camera angle, framing distance and background of <Picture 1>; do not add any person or object not present in <Picture 1>.
```

```
Hands and fingers remain anatomically correct with five fingers each; facial features stay consistent and natural.
```

## 六、soundscape 要匹配强度

环境音是最容易被忽略的维度，但它必须和 `intensity` 一致：

| intensity | soundscape 写法 |
|---|---|
| subtle | `Quiet room ambience, soft breathing, faint fabric rustle` |
| moderate | `Rhythmic movement sounds, quickening breath, soft fabric shift` |
| intense | `Fast rhythmic sounds, loud uneven breathing, sharp fabric movement` |

**禁止**描述画面里不存在的声音（如输入图是室内却写「窗外车流」）。

## 七、`title` 要求

- 2–4 个单词，感性、可识别
- **不含机位词**：POV / missionary / doggystyle / close-up / full shot
- **不同维度的条目要给不同调性的名字**，不要让 50 个名字听起来一样

参考命名思路（不要照抄，仅示例调性）：
- subtle + shy → `Tentative Glance`
- moderate + seductive → `Steady Rhythm`
- intense + ecstatic → `Breaking Point`
- remove_partial + longing → `Slipping Edge`
- pull_out + playful → `Wider View`

## 十、交付验证

收到后我们会自动检查：

1. JSON 可解析、50 条齐全
2. 每条 `motion` 与第三节表格逐字段比对，**任何一处不符即退回**
3. prompt 长度、固定句、禁用词
4. intensity 三档各抽 3 条人工比对，确认语言强度有真实差异
5. 五个维度的分布与目标完全一致

**然后会用 6 条实跑验证**（覆盖 subtle/moderate/intense 各 2 条 + 脱衣类 1 条），确认出片观感符合预期后才铺开 50 条。
